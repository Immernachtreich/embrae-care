Storage and precautions
Storage instructions
Store below 30°C in a cool, dry place. Protect from direct sunlight and excessive heat. Keep the cap closed when not in use.

Caution
Avoid contact with eyes. In case of contact, rinse thoroughly with water.

Note
Perform a patch test before use. Discontinue if irritation occurs.
