Logic
People who search for a quiz want a routine, not a skin type label, so the primary keyword is "skincare routine quiz". The secondary keywords are the plain questions a first-time visitor types (where to start, what routine fits, how many steps). The page copy avoids diagnosis words on purpose.
How the result is decided (developer)
All rules and weights sit in window.EMBRAE_QUIZ_CONFIG at the top of the page script, so they can be edited without rebuilding. They follow the v2 developer logic exactly: points are added from every answer, Everyday Support needs two evidence families (exposure Q07, comfort or barrier Q05/Q06, sun Q08), the highest score wins, ties between Start Simple and Build Your Routine are settled by the combined Q01, Q02 and Q09 signals, and a remaining tie goes to Start Simple. No single answer can decide the result. Scores are never shown to the customer. Add to cart: each product row adds that product, and the main button adds the whole routine. Start Simple adds its three products as three cart lines. The two four-step results add the Daily Urban Defense Routine bundle product (variant ID in the config), which Shopify should expand into the four products, with the saving and the free travel pouch, so all items land in the cart together. Fill the variant IDs in window.EMBRAE_QUIZ_CONFIG.cart.variants. Until they are filled, the buttons show a preview message. Analytics events (quiz_view, quiz_start, quiz_question_view, quiz_answer, quiz_complete, quiz_result_view, quiz_product_click, quiz_add_to_cart, quiz_restart) are pushed to window.dataLayer, ready for GTM.
