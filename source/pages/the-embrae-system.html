Reset

Cleanse & Prepare

Gently cleanse away daily buildup and impurities, leaving skin feeling fresh, comfortable and ready for the steps that follow.

Feels likeClear gel wash
UseMorning and night
Where it fitsFirst step in your routine

What it does for your skin

  • Removes daily environmental buildup and impurities.
  • Cleanses without unnecessarily stripping the skin.
  • Leaves skin feeling clean, balanced and refreshed.

The ingredients behind it

Niacinamide

Helps support the skin barrier and maintain a balanced-looking complexion.

Panthenol

Helps support hydration and leave skin feeling comfortable.

Ectoin

EMBRAE’s signature ingredient, selected to support skin hydration and resilience.

How to use

  1. Pump 1 to 2 times into wet hands.
  2. Massage over a damp face and neck for 30 to 60 seconds, avoiding the eye area.
  3. Rinse thoroughly with water and pat dry.
The idea behind Reset

A day in the city can leave skin feeling weighed down by dust, sweat and daily buildup. Reset is designed to cleanse without unnecessarily stripping the skin, leaving it feeling fresh, comfortable and ready for the steps that follow.

Protect

Shield & Protect

Make daily sun protection the final step in your morning routine, with SPF 50 PA++++ protection.

Feels likeCream-gel, non-greasy finish
UseMorning, as the final step
Where it fitsAfter moisturizer

What it does for your skin

  • Provides SPF 50 PA++++ sun protection against UV rays.
  • Offers a cream-gel texture with a non-greasy finish.
  • Designed for comfortable everyday wear.

The ingredients behind it

Rice Extract

Derived from rice and included in the sunscreen formulation.

Ceramides

Ceramide NP, AP and EOP. Used as part of EMBRAE’s ceramide system to support barrier care.

Ectoin

EMBRAE’s signature ingredient, selected to support skin hydration and resilience.

How to use

  1. Apply generously and evenly to face and neck, 15 minutes before sun exposure.
  2. Gently blend until fully absorbed.
  3. Reapply every 2 hours if exposed to sun. Use daily as the final step of your AM routine.

Sunscreen is one part of sun protection and does not provide total protection. Use alongside shade and protective clothing.

The idea behind Protect

In India, sun exposure is part of everyday life, not just the peak of summer. Protect makes daily sun protection an essential final step in your morning routine, helping you care for your skin before stepping out.
