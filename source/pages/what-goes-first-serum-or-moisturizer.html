What goes first: serum or moisturizer?

The order of your routine, explained once and clearly, so it becomes a habit.

It is one of the most common skincare questions, and the answer is short: serum first, then moisturizer. Here is why, and how to remember it.

The quick read

  • Cleanse first, then serum, then moisturizer.
  • In the morning, finish with sunscreen.
  • A simple rule: go from thinnest to thickest.

The simple rule

Apply products from the lightest texture to the heaviest. Thin, water-based products go first, so they can reach your skin. Richer products go on top, so they can hold everything in.

Why serum comes first

A serum is light and made to absorb quickly. If you put a rich cream on first, it can sit in the way. So after cleansing, pat Urban Defense Serum onto clean skin, 1 to 2 pumps, until it has absorbed.

Then moisturizer

Next comes your moisturizer. Climate Adapt Moisturizer goes on after your serum: 1 to 2 pumps, smoothed over your face and neck, then massaged in gently with upward circular motions.

Sunscreen, in the morning

Sunscreen is the last step, and it is for the morning only. Daily Shield Sunscreen SPF 50 PA++++ goes on after your moisturizer, about 15 minutes before sun exposure.

Do I need to wait between layers?

Give each layer a moment to settle before the next one. A minute or so is usually plenty. You do not need to set a timer. If a product feels like it is sitting on top of the skin, wait a little longer before the next step.

A simple day, start to finish

Here is how the whole thing can look, from waking up to bedtime:

  • Morning. Cleanse, serum, moisturizer, sunscreen.
  • During the day. If you are in the sun, reapply sunscreen every 2 hours.
  • Night. Cleanse, serum, moisturizer.

A few common questions

Does the order change at night?

No. The order is the same, you just leave out sunscreen.

What if a product feels too thick to go under another?

Try using a little less, or give it a moment to absorb before the next step.

In short

The full order is simple. Morning: Reset, Defend, Adapt, Protect. Night: Reset, Defend, Adapt. You will find a one-page view in the four steps explained.
